# Pilot Plan (draft)

Free pilot with a few clinics, after the prototype works with fictional patients.

## Setup

- **Clinics:** 2–4
- **Duration:** 4–6 weeks
- **Price:** free, in exchange for feedback and a short weekly check-in
- **Before starting:** consent process and data agreement in place (see [../06-privacy-legal/](../06-privacy-legal/))

## What we measure

| Metric | How | Target |
|---|---|---|
| Time on notes + instructions per patient | Physio estimate before pilot vs. app timing during | Save 1–2 h / week per physio |
| Programs sent per week | App data | Used for most patients with home exercises |
| % of generated text edited | App data | < 20% |
| Patient opens the link | App data | Most patients open it at least twice |
| Patient feedback | 3-question survey | Clearer than before |
| Physio would pay | Direct question at end, with a price | Yes from at least half |

## Weekly check-in questions

1. What did you use it for this week?
2. When did you *not* use it, and why?
3. What was wrong or annoying in the output?
4. Anything a patient said about it?

## End-of-pilot question

"From next month this will cost €__ per month. Do you want to continue?"

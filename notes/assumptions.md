# Assumptions to Test

From the pitch. Riskiest first. Update status as evidence comes in.

Re-ranked on 2026-10-18 for the new in-session focus (see [decisions.md](decisions.md)). Numbers 1–9 are kept from the first version so old notes still match.

## In-session focus (new, riskiest)

| # | Assumption | How to test | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| 10 | Physios often work with 2–3 patients at once, and lose time to patients waiting or calling them | Interviews ("During the session"), observation log | unclear (founder saw it in his own rehab) |
| 11 | A screen showing the next exercise reduces waits and interruptions | Observation before/after, pilot | unclear |
| 12 | Physios are comfortable dictating the plan in front of the patient at the start of the session | Interviews, demo reaction | unclear |
| 13 | Patients will follow exercises on a phone or tablet mid-session (sweaty hands, old phones, older patients) | Demo with patients, pilot | unclear |
| 14 | Under ~15 s from speaking to the first exercise on screen is fast enough | Demo, timing in pilot | unclear |
| 15 | Clinics have, or would buy, a tablet for the exercise area when patients can't use their own phone | Interviews | unclear |
| 16 | Physios will pay for less waiting and smoother parallel work (not only for less paperwork) | Interviews (money block), pilot end question | unclear |

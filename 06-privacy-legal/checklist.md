# Privacy & Legal Checklist

Health data is a special category under GDPR. None of this is legal advice; get it reviewed by someone qualified before the pilot.

## Before observing sessions

- [ ] Simple consent form for patients when I sit in on a session (no recording, no names)
- [ ] Agreement with the physio on what I can and can't note down

## Before the pilot (real patient data)

- [ ] Legal basis for processing health data identified
- [ ] Patient consent flow (who collects it: the physio, in the app?)
- [ ] Roles clear: clinic = controller, us = processor? → Data Processing Agreement
- [ ] All providers (hosting, speech-to-text, LLM) process and store data in the EU, with DPAs signed
- [ ] Providers do not use our data for training
- [ ] Data retention: how long are voice notes kept? (consider deleting audio after transcription)
- [ ] Encryption at rest and in transit
- [ ] Patient links: unguessable, expiring, no sensitive data in the URL
- [ ] Privacy policy in Greek
- [ ] Process for patient data access / deletion requests

## Medical device question

- [ ] Write down clearly why the product is a documentation tool (does not diagnose, does not suggest treatment)
- [ ] Check this position with someone who knows EU MDR
- [ ] Keep any future features (e.g. pain check-ins, alerts) under review; they may change this

# Message Templates

Keep them short and personal. Translate to Greek before sending and adjust the tone (more formal for clinics you don't know).

## Physiotherapist – interview request

> Hi [name], I'm Stylianos, a Computer Science student at the University of Cyprus here in Limassol. After surgery on my bicep I went through physiotherapy myself, and I'm now researching how physios handle notes and home exercises for patients.
>
> I'm not selling anything. Could I have 20 minutes of your time to ask how you do this today? I can come to your clinic at a time that suits you.
>
> Thank you!

## Physiotherapist – follow-up after interview

> Thank you for your time today, [name]. What you said about [specific point] was really useful. I'll let you know when I have something simple to show, if that's OK with you.

## Mentor request

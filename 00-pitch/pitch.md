# AI Assistant for Physiotherapists

*Working name to be decided*

**Concept summary:** A simple AI tool that turns a physiotherapist's spoken notes into a clear home exercise program for the patient and a professional clinical note for the clinic. Built first for small practices in Cyprus and Greece, in Greek.

---

## 1. The problem

**For patients.** In a physiotherapy session, the therapist shows the patient several exercises and explains sets, repetitions and what to avoid. The patient then goes home and has to remember all of it. Most people forget part of what they were shown, do exercises incorrectly, or slowly stop doing them. Recovery becomes slower, and in some cases the patient gets injured again.

**For physiotherapists.** After every session, the therapist has to write clinical notes and, ideally, prepare written instructions for the patient. This takes time they are not paid for. Many small clinics still rely on paper sheets, quick drawings or WhatsApp messages, because the existing software is expensive, complicated or not available in Greek.

In short: the physio has no time to write things down properly, and the patient has no reliable way to remember.

## 2. The solution

Think of the tool as an assistant standing next to the physiotherapist and writing everything down for them.

At the end of a session, the physio simply speaks into their phone, for example:

> "Maria, right knee, week three. Wall squats, 3 sets of 10. Heel raises, 3 sets of 15. Ice for 15 minutes after. Avoid stairs for now."

The AI then produces:

1. **A patient program** in plain Greek: each exercise, sets, repetitions, how often to do it, and what to avoid. Later versions will include a short video of the exercise, filmed by the physio during the session.
2. **A clinical note** in a professional format, ready for the clinic's records.
3. **Follow-up for the patient** (later version): reminders and a short daily check-in, such as "How was the pain today, from 0 to 10?", which is sent back to the physio.

The physiotherapist always reviews and approves everything with one tap before it reaches the patient. **The physio decides, the AI writes.**

## 3. Who benefits

| Who | What they get |
|---|---|
| **Physiotherapists** | Less paperwork, more time for patients, a more professional service |
| **Patients** | Clear instructions they can follow at home, in their own language, and a feeling that someone is following their recovery |
| **Clinics** | Better patient results, fewer drop-outs from treatment, a modern image |

## 4. Why now

- AI can now understand spoken Greek and turn it into well-structured text reliably and at low cost.
- Physiotherapists are increasingly open to digital tools, but most tools on the market are designed for the US or large chains, are English-first and are priced for bigger organisations.
- Small practices in Cyprus and Greece are underserved and ready for something simple and affordable.

## 5. Business model

- **Monthly subscription per clinic or per physiotherapist.** Patients use it for free.
- The value is easy to measure: if the tool saves a physio one to two hours of paperwork per week, the subscription pays for itself.
- Possible later additions: a larger plan for multi-therapist clinics, and partnerships with orthopaedic surgeons, sports clubs and gyms who refer patients to physios.

*Exact pricing will be decided after conversations with physiotherapists.*

## 6. Competition and how we are different

AI tools and exercise apps for physiotherapy already exist. Some large platforms let clinicians send exercise programs from a video library, and AI note-taking tools for therapists are starting to appear. This shows that the need is real and that clinicians will pay for a solution.

Our advantages:

- **Greek first.** Built for the language, the healthcare system and the working habits of Cyprus and Greece.
- **Voice first.** The physio speaks for 30 seconds instead of clicking through menus.
- **The physio's own exercises.** Patients remember *their* therapist showing *them*, not a stranger in a stock video.
- **Simple and affordable.** Designed for the solo physio or the small clinic, not for hospital chains.
- **Local presence.** We can meet clinics in person, support them in Greek and adapt quickly to their feedback.

## 7. Responsibility, privacy and safety

Health information is sensitive, and the product is designed with this in mind from day one:

- **GDPR compliance:** patient consent, secure storage, and servers hosted within the EU.
- **Human approval:** nothing reaches a patient without the physiotherapist reviewing it.
- **A documentation tool, not a medical one:** the AI only records and organises what the physio has decided. It does not diagnose or suggest treatments. This keeps patients safe and keeps the product clear of medical device regulation in its first stages.

## 8. First version

The first version will be deliberately small:

1. The physio records a voice note in Greek.
2. The AI produces the patient program and the clinical note.
3. The physio edits and approves with one tap.
4. The patient receives the program as a simple link, with no app to download.

Reminders, videos, pain check-ins and a clinic dashboard will be added only after real physiotherapists are using the first version and telling us what they need.

## 9. Plan for the next months

1. **Listen:** interview 5 to 10 physiotherapists in Limassol about how they handle paperwork and home exercises today.
2. **Observe:** with patient permission, sit in on real sessions to understand the workflow.
3. **Prototype:** build the first version and test it with one or two friendly physios, using fictional patients first.
4. **Pilot:** run a small free pilot with a few clinics and measure time saved and patient feedback.
5. **Launch:** start charging once physiotherapists confirm they would pay for it.
